Multiplication facts, one at a time

Practice the times tables from 1×1 to 12×12. The app keeps track of which facts you know and gives you more practice on the ones you don't. No clock, no rush.

Start practicing

How to use it

  1. Study the grid. Look over the multiplication grid. Hover over or tap any square to see its fact.
  2. Practice. Choose a number, a group, or Adaptive practice, then answer the cards. If you miss one, you'll see the answer, and that fact will come back again soon.
  3. Watch your mastery grow. Each fact moves up through five levels: New, Learning, Familiar, Strong and Mastered. Turn on "Show mastery" on the grid to see where you are.

Tips

  • Short, frequent rounds work better than long ones. One round a day is great.
  • Quick answers help a fact level up faster, but every correct answer counts.
  • 3×7 and 7×3 are the same fact, so they're tracked together.

Your progress

Progress is saved in this browser on this device. To keep a copy or move to another device, export it to a file and import it there.
